Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026

1. Core Philosophy

At Heliox OS, privacy is not a feature—it is the foundational architecture. We believe that your sentient interface should operate entirely under your sovereign control. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled when you use the Heliox OS ecosystem.

2. Zero Cloud Dependency & Local Execution

Heliox OS is fundamentally designed to run entirely on your edge device. By default, no personal data, system commands, or cognitive sync logs leave your hardware. All ambient AI processing, vision models, voice recognition, and system-level automations occur locally via your GPU/CPU, ensuring absolute cryptographic privacy.

3. Hybrid Cloud Mode (Optional)

We provide users the ability to explicitly opt-in to use frontier cloud models (such as Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, or Google Gemini) for highly complex logical processing. When Hybrid Mode is activated:

  • API requests are routed directly from your machine to the respective provider.
  • Heliox OS acts purely as a local interface client.
  • We do not intercept, store, log, or proxy your prompts or the responses through any central Heliox servers.

4. Telemetry and Analytics

Our commitment to maximum transparency means absolutely zero hidden telemetry. We do not track your usage behavior, your terminal inputs, the active agents spawned, or system configurations. If automated crash reporting features are introduced in future releases, they will be strictly opt-in and fully anonymized.

5. Open Source Verifiability

Because the core engine is open-source, you do not have to trust our words blindly. You can independently verify our data routing and privacy claims by reviewing the underlying Python code and execution nodes directly at our GitHub Repository.
